# Assessor issue-grounding prototype

An offline-only validator now represents unresolved assessment items with a source category, registered evidence location, and stated effect on the scientific claim. A proposed follow-on task must identify the decision it could change and the evidence it expects. Earlier unclassified issues remain readable. A proposed deliverable-ready finding does not change the frozen objective, select a candidate, or mark the open mission complete.

The private driver now supplies this grounding policy to both original-objective assessor paths. It registers frozen task, capability, and selected-evidence sources, while retaining prior unresolved records through a required locator and bounded retrievable JSONL parts. Host validation reconstructs those records exactly; the model proposes only new issues and changes, and a claimed resolution needs current-session reads of its prior line and newly cited evidence. Cumulative research history remains losslessly frozen with an untrusted episode/artifact locator. Current original inputs and selected evidence retain their complete-read requirement; historical parts are on demand, and an unread historical citation carries no evidence credit. Tests use synthetic sources and never inspect or publish private task material.
