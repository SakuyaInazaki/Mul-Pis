Within each partly retained file, remove all unlisted cases and any helper/import made dead solely by their removal. This is a reduction in executed cases, not a rename, skip, glob exclusion, or packaging of scenarios into loops.

The merge-recovery file also declares seven generated cases through its top-level `phase` and `status` loops. Both loops are deletion targets under the same rule: the retained pending-lift test exercises publication order, and the retained PID-reuse/cross-host test exercises unsafe lock takeover. Their four crash-phase and three owner-status permutations are not retained as hidden generated cases.

Within retained cases, remove further replaceable permutations: in `m07-failed-baseline`, keep only the `unknown` transaction state from its five-state loop; in `m08`, keep only the manifest-read-but-deliverable-unread variant; in `knowledge/experience`, keep withdrawal as the primary selection failure, drop duplicate capacity/tag failures in the first case, and reduce the X-version case to one pinned-scope method plus one bare-scope method to verify the actual dependency list. The pack-view case already verifies capacity, and the other experience case verifies tag applicability.
