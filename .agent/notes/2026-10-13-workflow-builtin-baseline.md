# Builtin workflow comparison baseline

The M07 evidence-handoff plan now accepts an optional `baseline` of `active-h` or `builtin`. Omission keeps the historical active-H comparison. In builtin mode the baseline arm begins a real M07 goal without a workflow H method, while the candidate arm binds its registered H method. The active evidence-projection policy pointer and its selected policy version are copied into the frozen template and both fresh arms. Only those copied policy files are excluded from protected G inputs.

Run receipts, the I decision view, the selection receipt, and development MetaEpisodes distinguish the actual comparison baseline from the H proposal seed. Candidate lineage and inherited necessary references remain attached to the seed H. Offline checks inspect the frozen goal and actual M07/M04 messages so a seed H cannot silently enter the builtin arm. The existing strict quality-gain admission rule remains in place; SDK estimated cost remains observational.

Validation: TypeScript typecheck and the isolated workflow fake test suite passed. This is a local workflow-mechanism check, not proof of general scientific benefit.

Follow-up regression: a legal short seed-H body can also occur as ordinary M07/M04 prompt text. The builtin-arm check now uses the frozen goal binding and the actual method-injection markers, without treating any matching body substring as an injection. The offline test first reproduced a false inconclusive arm and then passed with the corrected check.
