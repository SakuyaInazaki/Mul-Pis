# Private M04 draft recovery and evidence transport

M04 now keeps a host-written transaction checkpoint around each knowledge proposal. A structurally rejected draft and its exact private validation receipt remain unmerged; the same read-only research session receives format or structural feedback and may submit a corrected draft or choose no proposal. Before a possible merge, the checkpoint records merge intent. Only a returned published snapshot records merged. An exception after intent leaves the transaction unresolved for reconciliation, never an automatic replay.

The private campaign copies the complete bounded draft and receipt into an encrypted portable transaction, binds it to the M04 run and archived M07 task, and preserves it in the continuation history. Future import of that old candidate permits a fresh provenance check only for a fully authenticated no-proposal or rejected-draft record; missing, contradictory, or merge-possible receipts block that import. An older writer without the portable transaction cannot claim those details exist. A separate authenticated UNKNOWN quarantine can permit fresh independent research from the previously selected, revalidated tuple without importing or replaying the unresolved M04 work. The model must judge any new proposal from freshly returned evidence.

Archive collection now resolves same-goal/task archive variants by coherent monotonic control outcomes rather than filename order. Conflicting scientific file bytes or incomparable M04 effects fail closed. Historical research context exceeding the assessor's per-file bound is split losslessly into ordered UTF-8 parts with a byte-count index; every part remains subject to full-return read proof. No historical invoice, unknown operation, or scientific result is rewritten.
